# Literature review and contribution assessment

Review conducted 16 October 2026. This was a targeted, iterative discovery review, not a registered systematic review or a patent search. Search results were followed to original papers, institutional publications and primary documents where available. Search-engine coverage, English-language access and the availability of Georgian-language abstracts constrain the review. An absence of a located paper is not proof that no earlier paper exists.

## Research question after reviewing alternatives

**How do physical throughput, gross receipts and destination concentration give different accounts of recovery in Georgia's silicomanganese economy after the 2021–2022 boom, and which changes underlie the observed broadening of destinations?**

The study does not propose a new HHI, a new price index or a new Shapley value. Its contribution is a reproducible, case-specific empirical reconstruction through the complete 2025 calendar year, with explicit attention to baseline choice, route turnover and the local/national boundary.

## Alternative investigated and rejected

An initial Uzbekistan cotton-processing/water-footprint idea had close antecedents. Rudenko et al.'s 2013 paper, *The added value of a water footprint approach: Micro- and macroeconomic analysis of cotton production, processing and export in water bound Uzbekistan*, doi:10.1016/j.gloplacha.2013.09.007, already combines the cotton processing chain and water accounting. A 2024 Institute for Advanced International Studies paper also discusses virtual water embodied in yarn exports. Presenting the broad raw-cotton-to-yarn water argument as undiscovered would be untenable. The manganese study was selected after that check.

## Author's previous manuscripts

The openings of 58 PDF files in the author-provided research collection, including venue variants, were screened. The nearest topical manuscript concerns food/fertilizer import restoration through network-flow constraints. It uses different data, methods and questions. Another historical infrastructure manuscript concerns Asian railway timber. The present study independently assembles silicomanganese records and focuses on measured recovery and destination change; it does not reuse the earlier trade-restoration model or results.

## What can be claimed

- Original compilation, validation and case-specific analysis of the stated trade snapshot.
- An empirically documented sequence of concentrated rebound followed by contracting diversification.
- A useful distinction between aggregate and common-route unit receipts, with reproducible exact accounting.
- A contribution suited to discussion at a conference on Eurasian commodity frontiers.

## What cannot be claimed

- First-ever discovery of unequal exchange, Chiatura's crisis, market switching, export diversification or any accounting identity.
- A causal effect of tariffs, sanctions, protests, ownership or special management.
- Proven household welfare or ecological outcomes.
- A universal absence of prior work, patentability, a validated commercial market or a guaranteed/high-probability acceptance outcome.
